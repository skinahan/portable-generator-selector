# Load wattage provenance (Sprint 0)

These values in `loads.json` are **representative planning numbers** for MYR-RA-001 sizing logic — not surveyed measurements of every appliance SKU.

They are grounded in DOE Energy Saver guidance ranges and common residential backup-sizing practice. Each load also carries `sourceUrl` / `sourceLabel` for audit.

**Before public launch:** re-verify every wattage against primary sources (manufacturer nameplates / CPSC-adjacent generator load tables where applicable) and update this file with the audit date.

Central air conditioning is intentionally excluded from the catalog.
